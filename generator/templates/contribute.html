How tAr points work

One number for what you do here. It ranks the Members page and buys nothing. Every point is recomputed at each build from the archive's own records, so it can always be traced to a run, an act, a star or a post.

  • Never on your own run: you cannot star, verify, reproduce, or be counted as a commenter on a run you are credited on.
  • Importing a run, submitting one on somebody's behalf, editing records, reporting, moderating and holding a role pay nothing. Imported runs refuse acts, so only their authorship, stars and comments count.
  • Points once earned are kept: an invalidation changes the run's status, never anybody's points. A star taken back or a post the forum deleted takes its points with it, because the record is gone.
  • A game, group, system or category that an expert deletes as junk pays nothing: its record leaves with it.
  • Commenting counts for members only: a forum account that has never logged in here has no record to earn under.
  • The weights are provisional; the community settles them (Governance 2.7).

The rules

  • You cannot verify or confirm reproduction for your own run.
  • One reproduction and one verification per member per run.
  • You keep the points, even if an expert later overrules your contribution.
  • Contradictions open a case; nothing is removed automatically.
  • One Contributor badge, earned by the first tAr point; medals on the Members page for the week's and the month's top earner, for tens and hundreds of reproductions, verifications and firsts (hover one to read it).
