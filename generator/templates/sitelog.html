Site log

Everything done here by anybody holding authority, and everything done to a run after it was archived. Every entry states who acted, when, and why, and none of it is ever removed: an act of authority that cannot be read back is not accountable to anyone.

This page shows the last {{ LOG_WINDOW_DAYS }} days of each section (always at least the latest entries, and everything still open); the totals in the headings count the whole history, which lives complete and public in the archive repository and its git log.
