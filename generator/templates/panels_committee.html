{# The Steering Committee panel (/committee/): open name claims, whole-site experts, editors, recorded decisions #}

Steering Committee

Claims to held names, waiting to be answered. Approving hands the name over, renames the forum account and lets that person import their own runs; denying asks for a reason. Either way they are told, and the answer is public in the site log.

Checking who you are…

Open claims

Each row carries a masked form of the address the forum holds for that member, worked out as this page loads and kept nowhere. It is enough to tell whether it is the address that author would have; it is not the whole address, and nothing here or in the archive ever stores either form. The claim page tells them plainly that you see this much.

Appoint a whole-site expert

The widest scope there is: every system, every game, every group. It is the Committee's to give (Governance 2.5.3), and your name and reason are published with it. Members who already hold it are not listed.

Appoint an editor

The library's shape, nothing else: categories, groups, game identity, and which category a run sits in. No power over people and none over the runs themselves. A single Committee seat grants it, and your name and reason are published; taking it away is a Committee poll like any role removal. Members who already hold it are not listed.

Delete a member

For spam and test accounts (issue #61: this lives here, not on the member's page). Refused while they hold any role or authored any run: those have their own procedures. A sitting Committee member is the Founder's alone to delete, and the Founder is nobody's; neither is offered below. Your reason is public and permanent.

Record a Committee decision

The Committee votes on the forum; this writes the result into the archive, which is the only place a role is recorded. Point it at the post carrying the poll: the poll must be restricted to the Committee, public, and closed. Granting a role takes a simple majority of the votes cast (Governance 2.1.1), and so does every removal except a Committee seat's, which takes a hard majority, two thirds of every sitting member (2.3.5). Adding somebody to a forum group grants nothing.
