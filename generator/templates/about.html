About us

toolAssisted.run provides a platform for archiving and showcasing tool-assisted speedruns.

We are a community where casual viewers, speedrunners and TASers of all skill levels are welcome: from those just starting out to experts with years of experience. All types of TASes are accepted here, from casual superplays to runs that push a game to its absolute limits. In a nutshell, we wish to enable people to come together to enjoy and celebrate their favourite games through TASing.

How it works

  1. Go to the submit page. You need a forum account, and that is the only account there is.
  2. Select the game and category you are submitting a TAS for. Neither one there yet? You can create it, right in the form.
  3. Provide a video link and the rest of the run's information: authors, time, the movie file if you have one.
  4. Click Submit, and voilà: your TAS is showcased on the site, in the archive, and in the game's leaderboard from that moment.

From there your run can collect likes, be verified (somebody watches the encode and confirms it meets the category's goal, which is what ranks it) and, if you attached a movie file, be reproduced by somebody who plays it back and confirms it syncs. The worklist shows what is waiting.

Your own metrics

A category can be scored by whatever actually decides it: Score, Percentage completed, In-game time, Load-removed time.

Unranked boards

Play-arounds and experimental categories can be listed without a ranking, so a run that is not a race is not judged like one.

Permissive, not restrictive

  • Video-only runs are welcome. No movie file, no reproducibility requirement: the encode is the run.
  • The community decides which tools and emulators suit each game, not a central committee.
  • Tools like Cheat Engine, or one written for a single game, are allowed where the community accepts them.

A community, not a filing cabinet

Community guidelines

A strong set of community guidelines keeps this place free, open and speedrunning-oriented.

Forum

A full forum, ideal for members helping each other along in their TAS quest.

Discord

A gaming-first Discord server, where the talk stays on games.

Contribution points

Points for what you do here: verifying, reproducing, playing a movie back on real hardware. The board says who has done the most.

Every run is everyone's

TASes are a community affair: anybody can like a run and say what they think of it, on the run's page and in its forum topic.

Experts

Members become experts for the games they know, and weigh in when a verification or a reproduction is genuinely difficult.

Have a run to share? Submit it. It takes a few minutes, and it is archived the moment it arrives.
